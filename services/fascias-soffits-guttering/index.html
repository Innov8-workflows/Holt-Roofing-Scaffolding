Fascias, Soffits and Guttering

What's Involved

New uPVC fascias and soffits finish a roofline and keep the timber behind them out of the weather, and new guttering carries water away from the walls instead of down them. We fit white or black, and half-round or square gutters.

It's often part of a re-roof, but we do it as a job of its own too, where the roof covering is still sound.

What a Roofline Job Covers

  1. New uPVC fascias and soffits, in white or black.
  2. Half-round or square guttering, with new brackets, clips and downpipes.
  3. Where the felt has rotted at the eaves, the bottom courses of tiles come off and go back on over new membrane and an eaves support tray.
  4. Dry verge caps along the gable ends to finish the edges.
