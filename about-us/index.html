Meet The Owner

How Holt Roofing Started

Josh started with an apprenticeship at 17, working for a roofing company in Derby and going from labourer to roofer. From there Josh worked his way up with a few other firms, and started a small Facebook page doing repairs on weekends alongside it. Two years of that, and one of the people he was working for told him to stop using it as a safety net and go for it properly.

From that point it has been hard work and seven-day weeks that have built Holt Roofing Ltd into what it is today. Holt Scaffolding, the sister company, started more recently and is doing well too.

Holt Roofing Ltd is now a team of six, doing new roofs, repairs, flat roofing, chimney work, fascias and guttering, and dormers and roof lanterns, with joinery and repointing where they are part of the same job. Between them, the team's training covers working at height, manual handling, asbestos awareness and first aid.

"We are a very polite, approachable bunch of young lads with a lot of ambition and drive, and the only thing we want to do is give the customer exactly what they are asking for: a great roof at a great price, with no mess left behind."
