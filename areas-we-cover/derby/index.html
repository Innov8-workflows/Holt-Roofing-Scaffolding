Real Jobs in Derby

Roofs Across Derby, From Spondon to Hilton

Derby is the biggest place we cover, so these jobs are spread around the city and the villages just outside it.

A Derby re-roof with a dry hip

A full roof replacement in large concrete tiles on new breathable membrane, eaves support trays and 25x50 laths, with individual lead soakers and step flashing, a dry hip system and EPDM rubber detailing around the window.

Spondon, re-roofed in four days

In Spondon the old roof was stripped and a new one on in four days: membrane, 25x50 treated battens and large grey concrete tiles, a dry ridge and dry verge, Code 4 lead step flashing, individual soakers round the chimney and a new eaves support tray. We've also repaired a chimney and fitted a new rubber roof in Spondon.

A big rubber roof in Allestree

A large flat roof in Allestree, stripped off and rebuilt with 75mm insulation, new 18mm OSB boards and an EPDM rubber covering. The hips on the main roof were re-bedded and pointed in sand and cement with PVA.

Little Eaton: three valleys, two roof windows

Just north of the city in Little Eaton, a new roof in Ludlow tiles with three new dry valleys, two VELUX windows and dry hips and ridge, on treated lath and a breathable membrane.

Hilton: an extension for a builder

In Hilton we roofed a new extension for one of the builders we work alongside, in Rosemary tiles over membrane, support trays and 25x38 treated laths, with dry verge and dry ridge systems.

Alvaston, Morley and Darley Abbey

A re-roof in Alvaston. In Morley we changed 148 ridges from a wet bed to a new dry ridge and hip system. And something different in Darley Abbey: we ground out and repointed the whole front of a house.
