Full Re-Roof

Stripped back and built to last

Taken right back to the rafters and rebuilt: new felt, treated battens and interlocking tiles, with fresh ridge, verge and leadwork throughout. The finish is clean and uniform, sealing the whole home against the worst of the Derbyshire weather.

Conservatory Warm Roof

From cold add-on to year-round room

This old polycarbonate conservatory roof was freezing in winter and baking in summer. We replaced it with a fully insulated, tiled warm roof that is quiet, energy-efficient and finished with a smooth plastered ceiling inside.

Loft Conversion Re-Roof

A new roofline for a bigger home

As part of a full loft conversion we re-roofed the entire property, with new structure, membrane and tiles bringing the whole roofline up to a modern, weatherproof standard, ready for the extra living space created below.

Slate Roof Replaced

Slipped slates to a sound new roof

The old natural slates had slipped, cracked and gathered moss, with gaps opening up near the gutter. We stripped the slope and re-covered it in new slate-effect tiles, with fresh lead flashing round the chimney stack.
